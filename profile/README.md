## What we do

- **Mobile apps** - cross-platform iOS and Android with Flutter, from first screen to App Store and Google Play launch
- **Web apps and SaaS** - customer portals, admin panels and multi-tenant platforms
- **Backend and APIs** - scalable APIs, real-time features, payments and integrations
- **UI/UX design** - research, wireframes and design systems in Figma
- **QA and long-term support** - testing, releases and ongoing maintenance

One team covers the full cycle: project management, design, development, QA and handover.
